# Business brief

Build a commercial hypothesis before assembling a large team. For each shortlisted opportunity record:

- Buyer, buying trigger, and budget owner.
- Recurring pain and measurable cost of leaving it unresolved.
- Evidence source/date and remaining uncertainty.
- Existing alternatives and a credible reason to switch.
- Founder access, skills, assets, and constraints.
- Small paid offer deliverable within two weeks.
- Acquisition channel the founder can actually reach.
- Price, delivery cost, and assumptions behind the income goal.
- Strongest reason it may fail and a falsifiable experiment.

Suggested adjustable ranking: urgency 25%, distribution access 25%, founder fit 20%, time to paid value 15%, delivery economics 15%. Use 1–5 scores with explanations. This is a decision aid, not a probability of success.

Show downside/base/upside scenarios. Calculate contribution as revenue minus variable delivery/tool costs. Show founder hours separately with an explicit hourly valuation. Estimate LTV only with stated retention assumptions; don't call early guesses measured economics.

For the selected idea, define a 14-day experiment: identify accessible buyers, draft the offer, obtain authorization before outreach, ask for a purchase or pilot commitment, deliver manually if faster, measure willingness to pay and delivery effort, then continue/change/stop using thresholds chosen beforehand.

Never claim interviews, sent messages, agreements, payments, research, or product use without actual evidence. Label synthetic examples clearly. Do not confuse three pricing tiers with evidence that anyone will pay.
